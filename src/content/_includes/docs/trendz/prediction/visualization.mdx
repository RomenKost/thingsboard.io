## The model field

A model field holds the forecast of a prediction model, for every item of the model. You use it in a view like any
other field of the business entity.

- **When it is created:** Trendz adds the field when you turn on **Periodic refresh** for the model, as described
  in <DocLink product={props.product} path="prediction/monitoring#set-up-continuous-monitoring">Monitoring & Alarms</DocLink>.
  When you turn it off, the field is removed. When you turn it on again, the same field comes back, so saved views
  keep working.
- **Its name:** the name of the model, here **Site energy peak alert**. When you rename the model, the field is
  renamed too.
- **Where to find it:** in the field list of the view editor, under the business entity the model learns from,
  here **Site Energy**. Model fields are marked with a trend icon.

## Build a prediction view

### Step 1. Create a line view

On the Trendz home page, click **Create View**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-create-view.webp',
    alt: 'Trendz home page with the Create View button highlighted',
    caption: 'Click Create View.',
  },
]} />

Choose **Line**. The view editor opens.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-view-types.webp',
    alt: 'Create View menu open with the Line view type highlighted',
    caption: 'Choose Line.',
  },
]} />

### Step 2. Add the fields

Drag **Date** from the field list to **X Axis**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-step-date.webp',
    alt: 'Date field and the X Axis area with DATE (RAW) in it, highlighted',
    caption: 'Date on X Axis.',
  },
]} />

Open **Site Energy** and drag the model field, **Site energy peak alert**, to **Y Axis**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-step-model.webp',
    alt: 'Site energy peak alert field and the Y Axis area with the field in it, highlighted',
    caption: 'The model field on Y Axis.',
  },
]} />

Drag the telemetry the model predicts, **total**, to **Y Axis** as well. The chart then shows the measured values
and the forecast together.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-step-total.webp',
    alt: 'total field and the Y Axis area with total and Site energy peak alert, highlighted',
    caption: 'The measured telemetry on Y Axis.',
  },
]} />

### Step 3. Choose the item, the period, and the grouping

Drag the entity itself, **Site Energy**, to **Filters**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-step-filter.webp',
    alt: 'Site Energy entity field and the new Site Energy filter, highlighted',
    caption: 'The entity in Filters.',
  },
]} />

Click the filter and pick one item, **site-energy-001** here.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-filter.webp',
    alt: 'Site Energy filter open with site-energy-001 selected',
    caption: 'Pick the item.',
  },
]} />

Click the period and choose one that reaches into the future, as far as the forecast goes.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-date-range.webp',
    alt: 'Date picker open with a period from Sep 22 to Oct 6, 2026',

  },

Click **group by** and choose the same step as the model, **hour** here.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-group-by.webp',
    alt: 'Group by menu open with minute, hour, day, week, month, quarter, and year',
    caption: 'Group by the step of the model.',
  },
]} />

### Step 4. Build the view

Click **Build report**.

<ImageGallery images={[

    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/prediction/vis-build.webp',
    alt: 'View editor with the Build report button highlighted',

  },

<ImageGallery images={[

Click the share button and choose **Add on New Dashboard**, or **Add on Dashboard** for an existing one.

## Prediction field settings

Click the model field on **Y Axis** to open its settings. **Telemetry source** chooses where the forecast is read
from:

- **Product:** the forecast stored in Trendz.
- **Platform:** the forecast saved to ThingsBoard. It needs **Save telemetry** on the model.
